⚠️ "Damaged" or "Unverified" App?

Because we are in Private Beta, Apple might be extra protective. If you see an error, you do not need the terminal. Just follow these steps:

  1. Drag Bouquine to your Applications folder.
  2. Try to open it, then click Cancel on the error message.
  3. Open System Settings (the gear icon on your Mac).
  4. Go to Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section.
  5. You will see: "Bouquine was blocked..." Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
  6. Enter your password and click Open.

Note: This is exactly how apps like Discord and Spotify started before they were fully notarized. Once we exit beta and complete Apple's notarization process, this step will no longer be needed.

Local-First Architecture.

The Privacy Pledge

Effective Date: January 2026

Bouquine is designed to be a sanctuary for intellectual property. We believe your stories belong to you alone.

1. Your Manuscript stays Local

We do not use a cloud database. Your projects, characters, and research notes are stored in a private SQLite database on your own device. If the internet goes out, your studio keeps working.

2. Transparent AI Transmission

When you consult The Muse, only the relevant context is sent to your selected AI Provider (OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Anthropic). This data is never stored by Bouquine and, per major provider terms, is not used for model training.

3. Zero Hidden Tracking

Our beta includes no telemetry, no tracking pixels, and no background analytics. We find out about bugs only when you tell us about them.

4. Intellectual Ownership

You retain 100% ownership of everything created within Bouquine. We provide the forge; you provide the fire.
