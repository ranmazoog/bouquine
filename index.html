Installation Guide

Bouquine is in private beta and not yet code-signed by Apple/Microsoft. Follow these steps to bypass security warnings.

Why the security warning?

Code-signing certificates cost $99/year (Apple) + $400/year (Microsoft). I chose to spend my budget on building features rather than certificates for this beta. Once we launch publicly, Bouquine will be fully signed and notarized.

macOS Installation (2 Minutes)

1. Download & Install

Drag Bouquine to Applications folder

2. Open Terminal

Press Cmd+Space, type "Terminal"

3. Run Command

Paste command below and press Enter

4. Launch

Open Bouquine from Applications

xattr -cr /Applications/Bouquine.app
Why is this needed?

Bouquine is in private beta and not yet notarized by Apple. This command removes the quarantine flag macOS adds to downloaded apps. Once we launch publicly with Apple Developer certification (~$99/year), this step won't be needed.

1. Download Installer

Download Bouquine-Setup.exe

2. Bypass SmartScreen

Click "More info" → "Run anyway"

Follow installer, then open Bouquine

Bouquine isn't yet signed with a Microsoft code-signing certificate (~$400/year). Microsoft SmartScreen blocks unsigned apps by default. Once we exit beta and purchase a certificate, this warning will disappear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to start using Bouquine effectively.

📚 Core Concepts

What is "The Muse"?

The Muse is your AI writing assistant powered by Meta Llama 3.3 70B Instruct via OpenRouter. It helps you generate prose, continue scenes, and get unstuck. The Muse has access to your entire project: characters, world bible, synopsis, and previous chapters. It remembers everything.

Model: meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct:free
Provider: OpenRouter
Context: 131,072 tokens (can process ~100,000 words at once)

What is the Synopsis Workshop?

The Synopsis Workshop is a guided wizard that interviews you about your story (protagonist, goal, conflict, ending). It then generates a cohesive 300-word synopsis to guide your writing. Perfect for beating blank-page paralysis.

What is the Style Guide?

The Style Guide teaches The Muse your unique writing voice. Click "Analyze My Voice" after writing 300+ words, and Bouquine will extract your tone, pacing, and style. The Muse will then write in YOUR voice, not generic AI prose.

What is the World Bible?

The World Bible stores your worldbuilding: magic systems, geography, history, factions, and lore. The Muse references this to ensure consistency (e.g., if you wrote "magic requires blood sacrifice," it won't generate scenes where magic is free).

✨ Features

What is the Plan view (Corkboard)?

The Corkboard is a visual planning mode. Each chapter appears as a card showing its title, status, and word count. Drag cards to reorder chapters. Click a card to jump to that chapter. Perfect for outlining before you write.

What does "Write Prose" do?

"Write Prose" asks The Muse to generate a full scene (500-1000 words) based on your chapter title, synopsis, and characters. Use it to draft quickly. You can then edit, rewrite, or expand the AI's output in your own voice.

What does "Generate Beat" do?

"Generate Beat" creates a short outline (3-5 bullet points) for what should happen in this chapter. Use it when you know the chapter's purpose but not the specific beats. The Muse analyzes your synopsis and suggests plot points.

What is the Research section?

The Research section stores external facts: historical details, scientific concepts, real-world references. Example: "Medieval siege weapons," "How poisons work," "Victorian etiquette." The Muse can reference these to ground your fiction in reality.

Why is Focus Mode important?

Focus Mode turns OFF all AI features. Use it when you want to write uninterrupted, without suggestions or autocomplete. It's your sacred "just me and the blank page" mode. No AI, no distractions, no internet required.

What is the Consistency Checker (Shield Icon)?

The Consistency Checker (shield icon in The Muse panel) scans your chapter for plot holes, continuity errors, and contradictions. Example: "In Chapter 2, Sara had blue eyes. In Chapter 5, you wrote brown eyes." It's your story's fact-checker.

⚙️ Technical

Does Bouquine work offline?

Yes! Writing, editing, characters, and worldbuilding all work 100% offline. You only need internet when using The Muse (AI features). Your manuscript is stored locally in a SQLite database.

How much does AI usage cost?

Beta testers: FREE. We provide OpenRouter API keys with access to Llama 3.3 70B's free tier. Rate limits are generous (sufficient for multiple chapters per day).

After beta: If you use your own API key and exceed OpenRouter's free tier, costs are approximately:

  • $0.30-0.60 USD per chapter (R5-10 ZAR)
  • $6-12 USD for a full 80,000-word novel
Most writers stay within the free tier. Bouquine takes no cut—you pay OpenRouter directly.

Where is my manuscript stored?

Your manuscript is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Location:
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Bouquine/
Windows: C:\Users\[YourName]\AppData\Roaming\Bouquine\
Nothing is sent to the cloud. Your work never leaves your machine.

Can I export to Word/PDF?

Yes! Click the "Export" button (top-right) to export your manuscript as:

  • DOCX (Microsoft Word)
  • PDF (formatted)
  • EPUB (e-book)
  • Markdown (.md)
  • Plain text (.txt)
Exports use Standard Manuscript Format with proper indentation and headers.

The Privacy Pledge

Effective Date: January 2026

Bouquine is designed to be a sanctuary for intellectual property. We believe your stories belong to you alone.

1. Your Manuscript stays Local

We do not use a cloud database. Your projects, characters, and research notes are stored in a private SQLite database on your own device. If the internet goes out, your studio keeps working.

2. Transparent AI Transmission

When you consult The Muse, only the relevant context is sent to OpenRouter (Meta Llama 3.3 70B). This data is never stored by Bouquine. Per OpenRouter's terms, free-tier requests are not used for model training. You can verify this by reviewing OpenRouter's privacy policy.

3. Zero Hidden Tracking

Our beta includes no telemetry, no tracking pixels, and no background analytics. We find out about bugs only when you tell us about them.

4. Intellectual Ownership

You retain 100% ownership of everything created within Bouquine. We provide the forge; you provide the fire.
