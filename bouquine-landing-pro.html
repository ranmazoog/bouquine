The Privacy Pledge

Effective Date: January 2026

Bouquine is designed to be a sanctuary. Here is exactly how we handle your data:

1. Your Manuscript is Local

We do not have a cloud database. Your projects, characters, and notes are stored in a SQLite database on your personal device. If you uninstall the app, your data remains on your drive.

2. AI & Data Transmission

When you use "The Muse," text fragments are sent to the AI Provider you selected (OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Anthropic). This data is sent only to generate a response and is not stored by Bouquine. We advise reviewing the API data policy of your chosen provider (e.g., OpenRouter does not train on API data).

3. Telemetry

This Beta version does NOT include tracking pixels or analytics. We rely entirely on you emailing us bugs.

4. Beta Contact

We collected your email solely to send you the download link and update notifications. We will never sell your email.
